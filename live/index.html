Civory

A free search engine for public records. Ask a question the way you’d ask a person, like “How much does Canada spend on healthcare?” or “Did that border bill pass?”, and Civory searches official sources and answers in plain English, with links to the original documents.

  • Official sources from Canada, the US, the UK, Australia and New Zealand, plus data on 190+ countries
  • Government records first; news after, with a bias warning
  • Explains bills from both sides and says whether they became law
  • Document previews and citations for school work

Where I’m headed

The goal

I want to become a lawyer in private equity, working where deals, capital and the law meet.

It brings together everything on this page: equity research, corporate law, and building companies from nothing.

What I believe
  • Representation for all. Everyone deserves a capable advocate, whoever they are.
  • Rights for everyone. Rights mean the most when they protect people equally, and they should keep growing.
  • Liberty under the rule of law. Fair, predictable rules let people and businesses make their own choices.
  • Law as a tool for good. A legal system that works is one of the best ways a society helps its people.
